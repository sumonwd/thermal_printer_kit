## 0.1.0

- First release.
- Printers over Bluetooth Classic (Android), Bluetooth LE (Android, iOS), USB OTG (Android),
  installed printers sent RAW jobs (Windows), raw TCP network printers (all platforms), installed
  printer drivers (desktop) and the system print dialog.
- ESC/POS, Star Line (Star Graphic raster) and StarPRNT encoders: image, extra feed, partial cut and
  cash drawer.
- Discovery: paired and nearby Bluetooth printers, USB printers, driver printers, and a port 9100
  scan of the local network.
- Prints PDFs (rasterised at 203 dpi), Flutter images or raw command bytes.
